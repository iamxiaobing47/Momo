# Momo

[简体中文](README.zh.md) | [日本語](README.ja.md)

**Momo is a card-note plugin.**

It manages file names, titles, storage and formats for you, leaving only the note itself: every note is kept with tags and places, and revisited through different views and review modes, so you can meet your past self again in whatever way feels most comfortable.

Its philosophy is just one sentence:

> **Protect your attention, and thereby protect your emotions — put yourself where the leverage is greatest.**

When you wander in Momo, you are talking with hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands of voices from the past. In Momo, you are not alone. Every note you have written is borrowed from your past self, and from the people and things that walked beside you for a while; it is also a gift to your future self, and to someone who may one day read it.

Momo has been doing the subtracting for you since day one:

- **No more managing file names, titles, storage or formats.** Type a few words in the composer box,
  press Enter, and it is a note. What the file is called, where it lives, what it looks like — Momo handles it all.
- **Mark notes with tags and places.** Small marks like `#tag` and "Beijing" are all the organizing there is.
- **Random review is a stroll with your past self.** Nothing scheduled — just a quiet afternoon when an old note drifts gently into view.
- **A view for every way you like to read.** List, focus, globe, places — read however you please.

## FAQ

**Where is my data?**
All of it is in your own library, as ordinary note files you can open, edit and sync with Obsidian itself. The plugin keeps just one small ledger (what is in the trash, and your interface preferences), nothing more.

**Will uninstalling lose anything?**
No. Not a single one of your note files will be touched.
